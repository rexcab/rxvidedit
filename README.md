# rxwithcode • Motion & Asset Studio for Video Editors

A modern creator suite of browser-based motion design and asset tools built for video editors using **CapCut**, **Adobe Premiere Pro**, **DaVinci Resolve**, and **Final Cut Pro**.

## 🚀 Available Tools

### 1. [Number Counter Studio PRO](countNumber/)
- Smooth 60 FPS count-up and countdown motion animations.
- Lossless transparent PNG-in-MP4 alpha channel (0% background opacity).
- 35+ creator fonts, 3D extrusion depth, Gaussian glow, and outline strokes.
- Aspect ratio presets: 9:16 Vertical (Shorts/Reels/TikTok), 16:9 Landscape (YouTube), 1:1 Square.

### 2. Upcoming Tools (In Development)
- **Viral Subtitle & Caption Styler**: Kinetic text pop animations with keyword highlights.
- **Audio Waveform & Spectrum Visualizer**: Audio-reactive frequency waves with alpha video export.
- **Motion Lower Thirds & Social Callouts**: Animated channel subscribe buttons and handles.
- **Speed Ramp & Beat Sync Calculator**: BPM to video frame-rate timing sync.

## 📊 Admin Analytics & Uptime
- Dedicated visitor monitoring panel at `/admin.php`.
- Tracks daily, monthly, and all-time traffic with bot & UptimeRobot filtering.
- Automatic FIFO queue maintaining the latest 10 rendered MP4 videos.
